Questions

Before you send a quote.

Credit rules sit with RateMatch. Henderson Group runs this page and the campaign that brought you here.

Who is the credit licensee?

Credit services on this offer are provided by RateMatch under their Australian Credit Licence. The licence number is [PLACEHOLDER: RateMatch ACL number — to be confirmed]. Carmichael Henderson Pty Ltd (ABN 54 702 126 627) is not the credit licensee.

Does filling this form run a credit check?

This page does not run a credit check. A panel lender may do so later, with the notices the law requires, if you continue an application with them. Submitting a quote here is not a credit contract.

Which lenders will see my details?

RateMatch holds the panel. Live products today include home, car and personal loans. Names listed on this page (Pepper, NOW Finance, MoneyMe and further placeholders) are text labels, not a promise that any one lender will offer you a loan.

Can I get a business loan here?

Not yet. Business is marked coming soon. Choose home, car or personal if that is what you need now, or check back when RateMatch opens business products.

Do you charge borrowers for a quote?

There is no fee to send this quote request. Any loan fees, rates or charges would come from the lender you eventually deal with, and would be disclosed by them — not invented on this page.

What happens after I tap submit?

Today the button is a stub: it validates the form and shows a reference. When RateMatch’s endpoint is wired, the same payload will be posted there so they can match the panel and a lender can follow up.
