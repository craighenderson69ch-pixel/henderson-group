Before we begin.

If your question is not here, the private consultation is the place to ask it.

To request a reference in your industry and market, say so in the private consultation. We share it personally, under NDA — never as a public case study.

What is an exclusive lead book?
A signed allocation of inbound, qualified enquiries reserved to your firm in your market. It is not a shared list, a brokered name-file, or inventory that can be resold. The private consultation is how we write that book.
Do you publish results or case studies?
No. We do not publish named client results, pipeline figures, or dollar volumes. Anonymised, illustrative unit economics from exclusive engagements may appear as percentages. If a named reference is relevant to your industry and market, it is shared personally, under NDA.
Do you work with more than one firm in the same industry and market?
Not in the same market. Every engagement is exclusive — one firm in your line of work, in your city or country. If we take on your work, your direct competitors in that market cannot engage us for the duration of the contract.
How is Carmichael Henderson different from a lead-broker network?
Broker networks resell the same enquiry to multiple firms. We build the pipeline for you and only for you. There is no marketplace, no downstream partner, no aggregated database — the enquiry originates for your firm and is delivered directly to your CRM.
Why not just buy cheaper leads?
Cheap cost-per-lead often means enquiries that do not close. We generate demand your desk can monetize and judge the work on cost per sale — not the cheapest click.
What does the engagement look like commercially?
You name the vertical, the volume you can honour, and where the pipeline hurts. We design the campaign for that mandate, then return with a precise engagement quote — not a generic package.
Who runs my account day-to-day?
A named account lead — not a rotating support desk. You speak with the same person from strategy through campaign optimisation, and reporting is delivered under their signature.
Which regions can you support?
Active in Australia, Brazil, the UK, Europe, Asia (including Singapore, Hong Kong, Japan), the United States, Canada, and the Middle East. Other regions are considered case-by-case where the Insider Group network can deliver at standard.
How is confidentiality handled?
By default. Enquiries into this page are treated as commercially sensitive; identifying details are never shared publicly, on case-study pages, or with third-party vendors without explicit written consent.
