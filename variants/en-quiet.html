What does Carmichael Henderson do?
Carmichael Henderson is a done-for-you exclusive lead-generation firm. We win qualified customer enquiries for one client firm in one market. Your sales team closes. We are judged on cost per sale — never pooled, never resold.
Do you work with more than one firm in the same market?
Not in the same market. Every exclusive campaign is for one firm in your line of work, in your city or country. If we take on your work, your direct competitors in that market cannot engage us for the duration of the contract — so there is no conflict with a competitor.
How is this different from a lead-broker network?
Broker networks resell the same enquiry to multiple firms. We build the campaign for you and only for you. There is no marketplace, no downstream partner, no aggregated database — the enquiry originates for your firm and is delivered directly to your CRM.
Why not just buy cheaper leads?
Cheap cost-per-lead often means enquiries that do not close. We generate demand your team can turn into sales and judge the work on cost per sale — not the cheapest click.
Do you publish results or case studies?
No. We do not publish named client results, pipeline figures, or dollar volumes. This site carries no public case studies. In a private consultation we may share anonymised approach notes so you can judge how the work is built — never named results, never as a published case study.
