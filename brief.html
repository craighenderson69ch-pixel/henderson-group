Henderson Group, in one page.

Exclusive, done-for-you client acquisition for firms where reputation is the product. One client. One mandate. Never pooled. Never resold.

Henderson Group builds the pipeline for one firm at a time — in that firm’s industry, in that firm’s market. Delivery uses Insider Group campaign infrastructure. The enquiry originates for you and is delivered to your team only.

Who it is for Financial services, legal, real estate, healthcare, education, automotive, home services, professional services, and luxury & lifestyle.
Where Australia, Brazil, the UK, Europe, Asia, the United States, Canada, and the Middle East. Other regions case by case.
How you start You describe the buyer you want and how many qualified leads your team can handle. That brief is the design work.
What you receive A campaign designed for that buyer, then an exact quote and the cost per qualified lead — not a generic package.

The four steps

  1. You describe the customer, and the volume you can work.
  2. We design the campaign around that buyer — offer, audience, geography, timing.
  3. You receive an exact quote and the cost per qualified lead for that buyer.
  4. We deliver qualified enquiries up to your capacity, routed to your CRM or sales desk.

What this is not

  • Not a lead-broker network. The same enquiry is never sold to a competitor.
  • Not a public scoreboard. We do not publish client results or dollar figures.
  • Not a rotating support desk. A named account lead stays with the mandate.
  • Not an offer of engagement from this page. The site is an invitation to a private consultation.
